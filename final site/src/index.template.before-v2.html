THE SCULPTURE

Created in 2010, Circle Dance consists of eleven life-size figures holding hands in a circle. Installed at Brown University in 2012, the sculpture brings a sense of movement and energy to the campus.

THE INSPIRATION

Inspired by Henri Matisse’s La Danse (1910), the sculpture echoes the painting’s circular arrangement of dancing figures. Their linked hands can be interpreted as symbols of unity, movement, and shared experience.

THE ARTIST & MATERIAL

Tom Friedman is a conceptual sculptor known for transforming everyday materials. He originally modeled Circle Dance using aluminum roasting pans, preserving their impressions in the final stainless-steel sculpture.
